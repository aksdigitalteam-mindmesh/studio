# **App Name**: FitBoost

## Core Features:

- Calorie Tracker: Log daily calorie intake from meals.
- Water Tracker: Track daily water intake.
- Weight Tracker: Allow users to set a target weight, track their progress and optionally add pictures.
- Progress Dashboard: Dashboard display for calories, water, and weight, with a daily and historical view.
- AI Workout Plan Generator: AI-powered tool to generate personalized workout plans, with suggestions for intensity, duration, and type of exercises, for paid members, including options to grow specific body parts.
- AI Diet Plan Generator: AI-powered tool to generate diet plans with calorie and macro recommendations for paid members.
- Subscription Management: Manage membership and payments, including options to buy membership by earning fit-coins. Normal people can earn fit-coin by watching ads and it will give them 10 fit-coins for every ad and for every 250 calories they will get 25 coins and they can buy weekly membership for 750 fit coins
- Ad Integration: Free membership requires watching ads.
- Theme Toggle: Light and dark mode options for user interface.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Vibrant green (#7CFC00), symbolizing health and energy.
- Background color: Light gray (#F0F0F0), providing a clean and neutral backdrop.
- Accent color: Energetic blue (#007BFF) for interactive elements, such as buttons and progress indicators, calling attention.
- Body font: 'PT Sans' (sans-serif) for body text to ensure readability and a friendly tone.
- Headline font: 'Space Grotesk' (sans-serif) for headlines, providing a balance of modernity and clarity; to be paired with 'PT Sans' for body text.
- Use clean and simple icons to represent different food groups, exercise types, and tracking metrics.
- Maintain a clean, user-friendly layout that's easy to navigate. Key information, such as daily intake and progress, should be easily accessible.
